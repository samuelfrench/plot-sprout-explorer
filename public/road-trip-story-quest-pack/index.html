Parent steps

  1. Print the pack before leaving and clip one quest set together for each child.
  2. Use only passenger-safe prompts in the car and save longer writing for stops.
  3. Let the child point, circle, sketch, dictate, or write one line before asking for more.
  4. Put finished pages into the travel folder before changing locations.
  5. Use one share card after the car is parked, at a table, or during a calm visit.
