Parent steps

  1. Print and cut one small card set for the week instead of handing out all 30 at once.
  2. Choose one rubric criterion to score during a writing block.
  3. Let students point, circle, draft, revise, or share one sentence instead of demanding a full story every time.
  4. Save one finished card response per student as visible writing evidence.
  5. Rotate prompt cards by skill focus so practice stays varied without adding new software.
