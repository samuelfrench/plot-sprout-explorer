Parent steps

  1. Choose one postcard before the writing table starts so the note has a clear purpose.
  2. Keep delivery details separate from the writing page and handled by the adult.
  3. Let the writer point, sketch, dictate, write one line, or pass during optional sharing.
  4. Ask for one exact kindness detail before asking for a full note.
  5. Use one revision strip to polish the note, then let the family copy or deliver it offline.
