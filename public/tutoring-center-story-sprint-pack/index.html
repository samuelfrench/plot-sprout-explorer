Parent steps

  1. Print only the sprint pages needed for the day plus a few spare slips.
  2. Choose one routine and one page before the writer sits down.
  3. Let the writer point, sketch, dictate, write one line, or circle one keeper word.
  4. Use invented places, objects, and helpers instead of personal facts.
  5. Send one tiny practice slip home when the sprint ends.
