Adult guide

Story draft-checklist coaching

Composition Notebook Story Draft Checklist Adult Guide

  • Print the composition notebook draft checklist cards, take-home slips, and adult guide pages before the paper-only session.
  • Use one invented story draft at a time, then guide the writer through character, setting, sequence, useful detail, and clear-sentence checks.
  • Keep every example broad, fictional, family-friendly, offline, and led by an adult with pencil and paper.
  • Ask the writer to mark the composition notebook page lightly so the draft still feels workable.
  • Keep all notes inside the pretend story world with invented names, simple places, and safe everyday objects.
  • Finish by writing one next-draft pencil note so the writer knows the single change to try on the next pass.

Use one composition notebook story draft-checklist card at a time. Keep every choice broad, invented, paper-only, and screen-free.

Story draft-checklist routines

Choose the composition notebook draft-checklist routine

Character Check Circle

Making sure the story draft has a clear pretend character for the reader to follow.

  1. The adult asks who the main pretend character is on this composition notebook page: ____________________.
  2. The writer circles the first place that character appears in the draft: ____________________.
  3. The adult asks what the character is trying to do or notice in the story: ____________________.
  4. The writer adds one small pencil check beside the clearest character line: ____________________.

Setting Check Box

Finding whether the draft gives the reader a simple place or time clue.

  1. The adult asks where the made-up story moment seems to happen: ____________________.
  2. The writer draws a small box around the setting clue already on the page: ____________________.
  3. The adult asks which safe pretend place detail would help the reader picture the scene: ____________________.
  4. The writer writes one setting check note in the margin: ____________________.

Sequence Check Path

Checking that the story events move in an order a reader can follow.

  1. The adult points to the first event in the draft and asks what happens first: ____________________.
  2. The writer numbers the next event that follows on the page: ____________________.
  3. The adult asks which event feels like the last step in the story path: ____________________.
  4. The writer marks one arrow where the sequence needs a clearer move: ____________________.

Useful Detail Check

Choosing details that help the story instead of filling the page with extra pieces.

  1. The adult asks which detail helps the reader understand the character, place, or next event: ____________________.
  2. The writer underlines one useful detail already in the draft: ____________________.
  3. The adult asks which detail feels extra or unclear for this story moment: ____________________.
  4. The writer writes one small detail note beside the line to keep or rethink: ____________________.

Clear Sentence Check

Spotting one sentence that should be easier for the reader to understand.

  1. The adult asks the writer to choose one sentence to reread slowly from the draft: ____________________.
  2. The writer points to the words that tell the main idea of that sentence: ____________________.
  3. The adult asks whether the sentence says one clear thing or needs a pencil note: ____________________.
  4. The writer places a check beside a clear sentence or a dot beside one to revisit: ____________________.

Next-Draft Pencil Note

Ending the checklist with one focused note for the next draft pass.

  1. The adult checks the character, setting, sequence, detail, and clear-sentence marks on the page: ____________________.
  2. The writer chooses the one check that would help the next draft most: ____________________.
  3. The adult asks for a short pencil note that names the next change without judging the draft: ____________________.
  4. The writer writes the next-draft note at the bottom of the composition notebook page: ____________________.
