Adult guide

Connection coaching

Binder Ring Story Connection Adult Guide: ____________________.

  • Set out one binder ring connection card, blank paper, pencil, and a small paper ring mark for the adult-led fictional offline paper-only session: ____________________.
  • Label six paper lines detail, connects to, because, second detail, bridge sentence, and binder ring check before the writer begins: ____________________.
  • Choose one made-up story moment from a place like Nettlewick Hall or Mallowgate Yard and keep every detail invented: ____________________.
  • Tell the writer that pointing, dictating, adult-scribed phrases, or short independent writing all fit this family-friendly paper activity: ____________________.
  • Close by rereading the bridge sentence and binder ring check from the paper card only: ____________________.

Facilitation notes

  • Start with one small detail so the writer has a clear paper anchor: ____________________.
  • Ask what the detail connects to before asking because, so the story link forms in a calm order: ____________________.
  • Use the because line to explain the connection with one invented reason: ____________________.
  • Add the second detail only after the first detail and connects to line feel clear on paper: ____________________.
  • Treat the binder ring check as a quiet close to the bridge sentence, not a correction mark: ____________________.

Safety notes

  • Use only invented names, made-up places, pretend objects, and fictional story details: ____________________.
  • Skip any prompt that asks for personal facts, exact place facts, outside facts, or child identity clues: ____________________.
  • Keep the activity adult-led, offline, paper-only, and away from audience pressure: ____________________.
  • Keep every bridge sentence about the pretend story, not about a person outside the card: ____________________.
  • Use the binder ring check as a paper sorting mark for one fictional connection: ____________________.

Use one story connection card at a time. Keep every connection sentence broad, invented, paper-only, and guided by an adult.
