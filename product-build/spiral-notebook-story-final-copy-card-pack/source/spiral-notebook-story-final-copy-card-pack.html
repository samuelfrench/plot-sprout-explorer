Adult guide

Story final-copy coaching

Spiral Notebook Story Final Copy Adult Guide

  • Print the spiral notebook final copy cards, take-home slips, and adult guide pages before the adult-led paper session.
  • Start with one fictional revised draft, then guide the writer through title, first line, paragraph spacing, careful copying, gentle rereading, and final note work.
  • Keep every example invented, family-friendly, offline, paper-only, and guided by an adult.
  • Use the spiral notebook page as the clean copy space, with pencil marks kept light and easy to follow.
  • Keep all names, places, and objects inside the pretend story world so no private details are needed.
  • Finish by having the writer choose one proud sentence and one next-story note on paper.

Use one spiral notebook story final-copy card at a time. Keep every choice broad, invented, paper-only, and screen-free.

  1. The adult asks the writer to find the first sentence in the revised fictional draft: ____________________.

Paragraph Space Check

Leaving enough space between story parts so the final copy is easy to follow.

  1. The adult marks where the first story part ends on the revised draft: ____________________.
  2. The writer decides where a small space or new line should go on the notebook page: ____________________.
  3. The adult asks which next story part should begin after the space: ____________________.
  4. The writer copies the next part with clear spacing: ____________________.

Copy One Chunk

Copying a short piece at a steady paper pace without rushing the page.

  1. The adult chooses one short chunk from the revised draft for the writer to copy: ____________________.
  2. The writer reads the chunk once with a finger under the words: ____________________.
  3. The adult asks which words the writer will copy before looking back at the draft: ____________________.
  4. The writer copies that chunk onto the spiral notebook page: ____________________.

Gentle Reread Pass

Checking the copied page against the revised draft with calm adult support.

  1. The adult reads one copied sentence while the writer follows the revised draft: ____________________.
  2. The writer marks any missing or doubled word with a tiny pencil dot: ____________________.
  3. The adult asks what the sentence should say after the small fix: ____________________.
  4. The writer makes one quiet correction on the final copy page: ____________________.

Proud Sentence Note

Ending the final copy pass with a positive paper note and one next-story idea.

  1. The adult asks the writer to choose one copied sentence that feels clear and strong: ____________________.
  2. The writer underlines that sentence lightly on the spiral notebook page: ____________________.
  3. The adult asks what the writer wants to remember for the next fictional story: ____________________.
